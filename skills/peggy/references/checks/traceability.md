# Traceability: peggy

**What I compare.** Chains that the template's own structure implies, followed end to end. Meyer's
*Traceable* says consequences can be followed both ways; I am checking whether the links exist at
all.

**How the mismatch shows.** A chain that stops. Something at one end with nothing at the other.

## The chains

- **Stakeholder to check.** A category in G.7, a benefit in G.3 that serves them, a behaviour in
  S.2 that produces it, a criterion in S.6 that catches it failing.
- **Scenario to test.** A path in G.5, its detailed form in S.4, a test in S.6 from milestone 3.
- **Constraint to behaviour.** A restriction in E.3 or an assumption in E.4, and the behaviour in
  S.2 shaped by it.
- **Component to interface.** S.1 to S.2 to S.3, so that every part has behaviour and every
  behaviour has a way in.
- **Priority to plan.** S.5 ranking against the order of work in P.3 and P.4.
- **Dependency to risk.** Technology in P.5 against the risks in P.6.
