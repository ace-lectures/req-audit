# Terminology: peggy

**What I compare.** E.1 against every word the rest of the document actually uses. The glossary
is the only section that claims authority over the whole document, which makes it the only one I
can check against everything.

**How the mismatch shows.** Four shapes, and the last two are the expensive ones.

## The four shapes

- **Used but not defined.** A domain term carries weight in S.2 and appears nowhere in E.1.
- **Defined but never used.** An E.1 entry no section needs, usually left from an earlier draft.
- **Two words, one concept.** The same thing called two things in two books, so a reader cannot
  tell whether they are the same. This is the one that costs most later.
- **One word, two concepts.** A single term meaning different things in different sections,
  usually because two people wrote them.

## Also worth comparing

- **E.1 against the domain model.** From milestone 2 the glossary carries a class diagram. Class
  names and glossary entries should be the same vocabulary.
- **E.1 against the actor names** in the use case diagram, and against the stakeholder categories
  in G.7.
- **Definitions against usage.** A term whose E.1 definition no longer matches how S.2 uses it.
