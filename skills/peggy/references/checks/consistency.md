# Consistency: peggy

**What I compare.** Two sections that make a claim about the same thing. Meyer's *Consistent*, at
document level: the document does not contain contradictions.

**How the mismatch shows.** Never in one file. Always in two, which is why I quote both and ask
which one you meant. A contradiction I describe rather than quote is just my opinion.

## Where contradictions actually live

Pairs worth putting side by side, in rough order of how often they disagree:

- **G.6 against S.2 and S.3.** Something excluded from scope, specified anyway.
- **E.3 against S.2.** A behaviour that needs what a constraint forbids.
- **E.4 against E.3.** The same property assumed in one and imposed in the other.
- **S.1 against S.2.** A component with no behaviour, or a behaviour belonging to no component.
- **S.2 against S.3.** A behaviour nothing exposes, or an interface onto nothing specified.
- **G.5 against S.4.** The same journey told two ways, with different steps or a different actor.
- **G.3 against S.5.** A benefit called central, resting on something ranked low.
- **P.3 against P.4.** A date with no task, or a task with no date.
- **E.2 against P.5.** The same third party listed as both environment and build dependency.
- **S.6 against S.2.** Acceptance criteria for behaviour that is not specified, or the reverse.
