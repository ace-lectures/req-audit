# (E) Environment: frida criteria

One entry per section: what gets decided here, where teams close early, and what I ask. These
sharpen the three lenses in `cross-cutting.md`; they do not replace them. "Where teams close
early" is my note to myself, not a line I deliver.

Every question names an axis. None of them names what the answer should be. If the team could
paste my words into their document as a requirement, I wrote the question wrong.

## (E.1) Glossary

`parts/environment/E1.adoc` · milestone 1

**What gets decided here.** Which words are worth defining, and whose definition wins when people disagree.

**Where teams close early.** Definitions come from one stakeholder's usage, or from whatever a search returned, and the words the team argued about get the blandest entries.

- Where did each of these definitions come from? Whose word is this?
- Which of these means something different to a different stakeholder category?
- Which words did you argue about inside the team? Those are the interesting ones.
- What would a practitioner in this domain say you have got slightly wrong?

## (E.2) Components

`parts/environment/E2.adoc` · milestone 2

**What gets decided here.** Which parts of the world count as relevant, and where the edge of the environment is drawn.

**Where teams close early.** Only the systems the team already knew about. Anything long-standing enough to feel like furniture never gets listed.

- What else is in this environment that you did not list because it seemed too obvious to mention?
- Which of these could be swapped out, and would you notice?
- What existed before each of these, and what will replace it?
- Who owns each one, and what happens when their priorities stop matching yours?

## (E.3) Constraints

`parts/environment/E3.adoc` · milestone 2

**What gets decided here.** What gets treated as non-negotiable.

**Where teams close early.** A default gets recorded as a law. Nobody asks who could lift a constraint, so nobody finds out that half of them are softer than they look.

- Who could lift this, and have you asked them?
- What is this constraint protecting? Is that thing still true?
- If it vanished tomorrow, what would you build instead?
- Which of these did you inherit without ever checking?

## (E.4) Assumptions

`parts/environment/E4.adoc` · milestone 2

**What gets decided here.** What to assume rather than go and establish, and how much rests on each one.

**Where teams close early.** Assumptions get chosen for convenience early on and are never revisited, including the ones that quietly became load bearing.

- What made this worth assuming rather than finding out?
- How would you discover it was false, and when?
- What does this section look like written by somebody pessimistic?
- Which one of these, if wrong, costs you the most?

## (E.5) Effects

`parts/environment/E5.adoc` · milestone 1

**What gets decided here.** Which effects count as worth recording, and whose environment is being considered.

**Where teams close early.** Only the intended ones, and only the good ones. The effects on people who did not ask for the system go unwritten.

- Which of these did you intend, and which simply follow?
- Who experiences this effect without having asked for it?
- What does the environment look like after a year of this running?
- What effect would you be uncomfortable writing down?

## (E.6) Invariants

`parts/environment/E6.adoc` · milestone 1

**What gets decided here.** What must be preserved, and who was preserving it before the system existed.

**Where teams close early.** The obvious data invariants get written and nothing social, physical or procedural does. Invariants currently held up by a person noticing are invisible.

- What must never be true here, whatever happens? Is that written down?
- Which of these matter to a person rather than to the data?
- What is held together today by somebody noticing, that the system is about to take over?
- Which of these would you not discover was broken for six months?
